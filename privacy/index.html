01

Data in Fupori

Fupori stores the information you enter or create while using the app, including habit names and schedules, reminders, completion history, companion progress, achievements, and care rewards. It also saves app preferences such as your display name, theme, notification choice, and first-use date.

This information is stored on your device. The production app uses Apple's CloudKit to sync habit and progress records through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. Apple stores that synced data in your iCloud account. The developer cannot view your private database in the CloudKit developer portal.

The app's widget reads a small snapshot shared locally between the app and its widget. Habit reminders are scheduled as notifications on your device.

02

Services we use

App analytics

In the production app, a third-party analytics service receives an app launch event with the app environment. Its software may also add an identifier, event time, and app or device details. We use this to understand app launches. Fupori does not intentionally send habit names or completion history to this service. Automatic screen capture, control capture, and session replay are disabled.

Error and performance diagnostics

A third-party diagnostics service receives error and crash reports, logs, performance traces, and profiling data so we can diagnose problems. Reports can include device and app details, an IP address, and information contained in error messages or logs. Diagnostic reports may contain personal information.

Apple iCloud

CloudKit handles sync for the app's private iCloud data. You can use Fupori without an app account; iCloud sync depends on your device's Apple Account and iCloud settings.

Our analytics and diagnostics providers process information for the purposes described above. You can read Apple's privacy policy for information about iCloud.

03

Your choices

You can edit or delete habits in Fupori. You can turn off habit reminders in the app or change notification permission in iOS Settings. You can manage Fupori's iCloud access in your device's iCloud settings.

The current app has no in-app switch for analytics or diagnostics. To stop new data from being sent by the app, stop using it or remove it from your device. If you have a privacy request about data already sent, use the contact link below.

04

Retention and deletion

Removing Fupori deletes its local app data from that device. If you used iCloud sync, copies can remain in your private iCloud storage and on your other devices. You can manage that storage through your Apple Account's iCloud settings.

Our analytics and diagnostics providers keep information according to their service settings and applicable retention policies. Contact us to ask about access to or deletion of information held through those services. We may need enough information to find the relevant records, and some requests may be limited by what can be identified or by legal requirements.
